## Filters

Start with:
- Experience level: Entry + Intermediate
- Payment verified: Yes
- Client history: at least 1 hire preferred
- Budget: $50–$500 for first jobs
- Avoid: huge vague projects, unpaid tests, clients with bad reviews

## Best-fit job signals

Apply when the client says:
- “Need this quickly”
- “Looking for someone to audit/review”
- “Need help with Zapier/Make”
- “Need lead list”
- “Need landing page feedback”
- “Need AI workflow”
- “Need content repurposed”

Skip when:
- They want a full SaaS built for $50
- They ask for free work
- Requirements are vague and huge
- They need advanced custom API work before we have scope

## Daily quota

Before first sale:
- 20 proposals/day
- 10 direct prospects/day
- 1 daily public update only

After first sale:
- Fulfill fast
- Ask for review/testimonial
- Raise minimum package price
